# Start Here

This is the {{WORKSPACE_NAME}} second brain. Obsidian is its configured human interface and QMD is its agent retrieval layer. Markdown files remain canonical and usable with any editor; agents follow `AGENTS.md`.

## The Loop

1. Begin ongoing work from the relevant page under `projects/` or `domains/`.
2. Put unsynthesized local source material in `raw/`.
3. Ask an agent to compare new material with existing knowledge and propose an ingestion review packet.
4. Approve, reject, or refine the proposed changes.
5. Navigate through `index.md` and meaningful wiki links.
6. Capture durable decisions and discoveries at meaningful milestones.
7. Periodically ask an agent to lint retrieval and knowledge quality without automatically rewriting the graph.

## Ownership

- This knowledge base owns durable synthesis.
- `../braingraph.json` identifies external systems and what they own.
- Live status must be verified in its owning system.
- Raw sources and reports are evidence, not automatically current truth.
- Search indexes are disposable discovery aids.

## Useful Requests

- `Orient me to the [project or domain]. Verify volatile state in its live owner.`
- `Ingest the new material in raw/. Propose changes before applying them.`
- `What does this workspace know about [topic]? Cite the supporting pages and surface conflicts.`
- `What important questions about [topic] remain unanswered?`
- `Update the [project] hub with the durable decisions from this work.`
- `Lint the knowledge graph. Report issues without reorganizing it.`
