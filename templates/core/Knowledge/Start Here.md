# Start Here

This is the {{WORKSPACE_NAME}} second brain. Its current purpose, scope, sensitivity, and maintenance boundary are declared in `../braingraph.json`. Obsidian is its configured human interface and QMD is its agent retrieval layer. Markdown files remain canonical and usable with any editor; agents follow `AGENTS.md`.

## The Loop

1. Begin ongoing work from the relevant page under `projects/` or `domains/`.
2. Put unsynthesized local source material in `raw/`.
3. Ask an agent to compare new material with existing knowledge and determine what is already authorized by your request or the manifest's maintenance policy.
4. When changes are not already authorized, approve, reject, or refine the agent's ingestion review packet.
5. Navigate through `index.md` and meaningful wiki links.
6. Capture durable decisions and discoveries at meaningful milestones.
7. During other substantive work, expect agents to propose a compact knowledge-maintenance packet when durable understanding changed.
   If `braingraph.json` delegates a narrow routine-maintenance scope, agents may apply changes inside that scope and must report them.
8. Periodically ask an agent to lint retrieval and knowledge quality without automatically rewriting the graph.

## Ownership

- This knowledge base owns durable synthesis.
- `../braingraph.json` identifies external systems and what they own.
- `../braingraph.json` also owns the workspace sensitivity and routine knowledge-maintenance boundary.
- Live status must be verified in its owning system.
- Raw sources and reports are evidence, not automatically current truth.
- Search indexes are disposable discovery aids.

## Useful Requests

- `Orient me to the [project or domain]. Verify volatile state in its live owner.`
- `Ingest the new material in raw/. Propose changes before applying them.`
- `What does this workspace know about [topic]? Cite the supporting pages and surface conflicts.`
- `What important questions about [topic] remain unanswered?`
- `Update the [project] hub with the durable decisions from this work.`
- `Before finishing, identify durable knowledge from this work and propose any vault updates that are not already authorized.`
- `Lint the knowledge graph. Report issues without reorganizing it.`
